# 🐾 KEEPER 🐾

**Your schedule has a guard dog now.**

He don't bark for nothing. Black pit, blue eyes, electric aura. He watches your calendar while you sleep.

No inspirational quotes. No productivity BS. Just your plans - locked, synced, and guarded.

### HOW IT WORKS
- CLICK any day box -> type 9am gym / 3pm pull-up / 11pm lock in -> ENTER to save
- SCAN the QR -> opens ntfy.sh/keeper-xxxx -> tap Subscribe -> phone blows up when you miss something
- PRINT from top bar - exact copy of your calendar with all edits
- SYNC anywhere - add ?sync=keeper-xxxx to URL, same topic everywhere
